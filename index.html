01A LETTER FOR YOU

Selamat Hari JadiKakak Sayang,

I like getting to know you, even knowing that maybe I’ll never truly know every part of you. And honestly, I think that’s okay, karena sampai kapan pun, mungkin akan selalu ada sisi dari seseorang yang baru kita kenal seiring waktu.

There are still so many sides of you that I’ve only recently discovered. Some are easy to love, while others are things I’m still learning to understand. But maybe that's what loving someone is supposed to be and still want to discover a little more about them every day.

I’ve learned that being loved isn’t only about feeling happy with someone. Sometimes, love also exists in the moments when we’re tired, when we misunderstand each other, or when we don’t even know how to explain what we’re feeling. And I want us to keep learning how to do that.

I don’t know what the months ahead will bring, but I know what I want to do today; choose you. Among all the things we still have to learn, I still want to walk beside you. Aku masih mau memperbaiki semuanya ketika ada yang salah, membicarakan hal-hal yang terasa berat, and find our way back to each other whenever we somehow lose our way.

Maybe home isn’t always a place. Somewhere between all our conversations, laughter, misunderstandings, missing each other, and all the ordinary days we’ve shared, I found something that feels a little like that with you. I just want to be one of the places you can come to when the world feels too loud. ♡

with lots of love,
someone who cares about you ♡
